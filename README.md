# 🍔 Food Delivery Data Visualization Portfolio

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive visualization portfolio based on a Food Delivery Business Performance Dataset. It uses Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn to explore business trends, compare categories, examine numerical distributions, and identify meaningful relationships among important performance variables.

The analysis focuses on orders, revenue, marketing spend, discounts, delivery time, customer ratings, repeat customers, cities, cuisines, weather conditions, and order channels.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze monthly changes in orders and revenue.
* Compare business performance across cities and cuisines.
* Examine the relationship between marketing spend and revenue.
* Investigate the relationship between delivery time and customer ratings.
* Study the distributions of important numerical variables.
* Compare delivery performance under different weather conditions.
* Analyze customer ratings and usage across order channels.
* Identify relationships between numerical variables using a correlation heatmap.
* Summarize the most important data-driven findings.

## 📂 Dataset Information

The dataset contains **360 records and 16 original columns**.

Important variables include:

* Date, Month, and Day
* City and Cuisine
* Order Channel and Weather
* Orders and Average Order Value
* Revenue and Marketing Spend
* Discounts
* Average Delivery Time
* Customer Rating
* Repeat Customer Percentage

The dataset contains no missing values or duplicate records.

## 📊 Visualizations Included

The portfolio contains the following visualizations:

1. Monthly Orders Trend – Line Plot
2. Monthly Revenue Trend – Line Plot
3. Total Revenue by City – Bar Chart
4. Total Orders by Cuisine – Bar Chart
5. Marketing Spend vs Revenue – Scatter Plot
6. Delivery Time vs Customer Rating – Scatter Plot
7. Revenue Distribution – Histogram with KDE
8. Delivery Time by Weather – Box Plot
9. Customer Ratings by Order Channel – Violin Plot
10. Order-Channel Frequency – Count Plot
11. Average Customer Rating by Weather – Bar Chart
12. Numerical Variable Correlation – Heatmap

Each visualization includes a title, axis labels, appropriate formatting, and a brief data-based interpretation.

## 🔍 Key Findings

* June recorded the highest number of orders, while December generated the highest total revenue.
* Bengaluru and Delhi were the leading cities based on total revenue.
* Healthy cuisine received the highest total number of orders.
* Marketing spend had only a weak positive relationship with revenue.
* Longer delivery times were strongly associated with lower customer ratings.
* Rainy weather recorded the longest average delivery time and the lowest average customer rating.
* The App was the dominant order channel and achieved the highest average customer rating.
* Higher customer ratings were strongly associated with a greater percentage of repeat customers.
* Order volume had a strong positive relationship with revenue.
